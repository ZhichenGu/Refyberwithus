Refyber With Us

Reducing textile waste in Hong Kong — one fiber, one project, one community at a time.

Our Mission

Refyber is a sustainability initiative by two high school students in Hong Kong. We transform textile waste — from discarded clothing to microscopic microfibers — into resources that serve our community and inspire environmental awareness.
